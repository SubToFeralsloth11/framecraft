FAQ

Do I need to install anything?

No. FrameCraft runs in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox on Mac, Windows, Chromebook, iPhone, iPad and Android. On a phone you edit with touch: pinch to zoom, two fingers to pan.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything is processed on your device and your files never leave it.

How do I activate Pro?

After checkout you get a license key by email. Click Upgrade to Pro in the editor and paste it in.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
